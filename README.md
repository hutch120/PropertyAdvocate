# PropertyAdvocate

PropertyAdvocate provides property-matching reports as a service to buyers advocates.
Buyers advocates (also known as buyers agents) are engaged by home buyers and investors to
find, assess and negotiate the purchase of a property, but they need to sift through large
volumes of listings to find properties that genuinely fit each client's brief. PropertyAdvocate
supplies the data and reporting layer that supports that work.

## What we do

- **Capture client requirements** — budget, location preferences, household composition and
  lifestyle needs for each advocate's customer (see [Customers/](Customers)).
- **Track candidate properties** — current listings the advocate is considering (see
  [Properites/](Properites)).
- **Match customers to properties** and generate polished, white-labelled reports that advocates
  can present directly to their clients, explaining why a given property suits their brief (see
  the sample report and formatting in [business/customer-1-report.html](business/customer-1-report.html)).

## Target customers

Our customers are buyers advocacy firms, not the end home buyer. See
[business/davidson-property-advocates-analysis.md](business/davidson-property-advocates-analysis.md)
for an example of the kind of firm we'd sell this reporting service to, and the broader set of
