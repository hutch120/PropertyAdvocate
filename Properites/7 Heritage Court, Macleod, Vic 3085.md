Springthorpe Lifestyle in Cul-De-Sac Position.
7 HERITAGE COURT, MACLEOD
Custom built with focus on privileged family lifestyle and effortless entertaining credentials, this expansive domain celebrates modern comfort in a tranquil cul-de-sac position within the coveted natural environs of Springthorpe - just a short walk to the Country Club, recreation reserve, playground and native parkland trails.

Immediately welcoming in contemporary style, the grand entry hall introduces a formal lounge and a casual living room. The expansive kitchen is poised for culinary enjoyment equipped with a 900mm cooker, concealed appliance bench and ample cabinetry, all anchored by a stone-topped breakfast bench servery orientated for connection with the open concept dining which seamlessly extends to the covered patio inviting alfresco occasions amidst the low maintenance garden boasting significant space, ideal for kids play areas, further shed storage or pool.

Upstairs hosts accommodation comprising three robed bedrooms serviced by the central family bathroom, accompanied by the grand master suite complete with walk in robe and sleek ensuite. Further complements include a double remote garage with workshop/storage space plus drive through access for extra vehicle/trailer/boat and internal entry, full size laundry, ground floor powder room, ducted heating and cooling, ducted vacuum and alarm.

Set amongst picturesque parklands, native wildlife reserves & walking tracks in a family favoured locale, additional lifestyle advantages include exclusive use of the Country Club swimming pool, tennis courts and gymnasium. Situated with convenience surrounded by Polaris shopping & trams, Macleod Village and trains, Latrobe University, Austin Hospital major medical precinct and easy arterial access to be enhanced by the North East Link currently under construction.
