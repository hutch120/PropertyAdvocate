38 MACLEOD PARADE, MACLEOD
What an exciting opportunity to rebuild, create your dream family home or explore development potential (STCA) while combining outstanding everyday convenience within a peaceful, family-focused setting. Rarely found on a generous 610m2 (approx.) Corner allotment in the tightly held Victoria Cross Estate, this property enjoys an exceptional location just a short stroll from Macleod Village, Macleod Train Station, local shops, cafes, parks and schools, with La Trobe University also nearby.

For those considering redevelopment, two professionally prepared dual-occupancy concept plans are available, together with an arborist report and surveyor's report, providing valuable information to assist prospective purchasers in exploring the site's potential.

The existing residence comprises three bedrooms, including a main bedroom with ensuite, two living areas, a functional kitchen with meals area, and a double garage with driveway access. While requiring extensive renovation, the home provides a solid footprint for those looking to restore or update, with timber-lined ceilings, established surrounds and a secure rear yard adding further appeal.

Whether you choose to renovate the existing home, build your dream family residence or capitalise on the prized corner allotment, the opportunities are considerable.

With Macleod Village and the train station within easy walking distance, along with shops, cafes, parks, quality schools and La Trobe University nearby, this is a location that delivers the convenience of connected living while retaining the peaceful, family-friendly character that makes Victoria Cross Estate so tightly held.
