Grand family home offering the charm of days gone by, with all the conveniences of modern life
22 DONALDSON STREET, IVANHOE • DATE PUBLISHED: 29 SEP 2026
Art Deco character runs throughout this home, from the beautiful stained glass windows to the formal living areas downstairs, where pelmeted windows and luxurious window coverings add a classic finish. These rooms are formal yet cosy, warmed by both an ornate brick fireplace and a gas log fire.

At the rear, a modern family room with floor to ceiling windows fills with natural light and opens onto a generous outdoor area with room for a dining setting, a trampoline, or both.

The well proportioned kitchen features a Miele oven and gas cooktop, stone benchtops, a country style sink, an island bench, a pantry and space for the largest plumbed fridge.

There are four spacious bedrooms, including a main bedroom with walk in robe and ensuite. Period style bathrooms upstairs and downstairs are joined by a guest powder room and a full laundry with direct access to the rear garden. Ducted heating and cooling keep the home comfortable year round.

The front and rear gardens are designed to deliver colour through every season, kept green by a large rainwater tank. A high brick fence provides privacy, and an enclosed garage plus a carport, both with remote controlled roller doors, offer secure parking.

Just moments to Cremean Park and playground, and within walking distance of Ivanhoe Central, the Village shops and dining, Ivanhoe train station, Ivanhoe Primary, Ivanhoe Girls' Grammar, Ivanhoe Grammar, Ivanhoe Aquatic Centre and the Ford Park sporting reserves. The Austin Hospital precinct, La Trobe University and major arterial roads are all close by.
