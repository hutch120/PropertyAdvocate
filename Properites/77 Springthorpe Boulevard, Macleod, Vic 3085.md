Magnificent Family Sanctuary in Springthorpe
77 SPRINGTHORPE BOULEVARD, MACLEOD
EXPRESSIONS OF INTEREST (UNLESS SOLD PRIOR)

Architecturally designed to embrace principles of natural light and seamless indoor-outdoor connection, this domain of timeless style delivers exceptional family comfort and outstanding entertaining appeal, spanning an expansive 959m2 (approx.) landholding, proudly positioned within the coveted leafy environs of Springthorpe.

A soaring double-height entrance creates an immediate sense of sophistication, revealing a generously proportioned floorplan expertly crafted by renowned Rustic Builders and freshly enhanced with new paint and carpets throughout.

At the heart of the home, the bespoke kitchen appointed with premium Smeg appliances and a breakfast bench servery, orientated for connection with open-plan meals and living. These spaces flow effortlessly onto a covered deck, creating the ideal setting for alfresco occasions amidst the sprawling established garden. A substantial separate lounge offers superb versatility, functioning equally well as a formal living and dining room, rumpus, or dedicated media retreat.

Upstairs hosts accommodation comprising three generously sized bedrooms with built-in robes, serviced by the family bathroom complete with separate bath and shower. Connected by an impressive suspended boardwalk, the primary suite boasts a walk-in wardrobe and sleek ensuite with double vanity.

Additional highlights include a dedicated study, full-sized laundry, ground-floor powder room, remote double garage with internal access, ducted heating, refrigerated cooling and supplementary reverse-cycle systems for year-round comfort and efficiency.

Situated in a family favoured locale, residents enjoy exclusive access to the Springthorpe Country Club facilities, including a swimming pool, gymnasium and tennis courts. Surrounded by scenic parklands and walking trails, this sublime sanctuary is conveniently located close to Macleod Train Station and charming village cafes, Polaris shopping square, tram services, La Trobe University, Austin Hospital major medical district and easy access to key arterials with further connectivity to come via the North East Link project.
